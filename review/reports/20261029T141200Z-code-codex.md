### 1. [major] E13’s chain budget joins separate sequences before scoring them
- where: `loud/cut.py:70`
- evidence: `torch.cat([capture.x.flatten(), capture.y.flatten()[-1:]])` drops every non-final sequence’s last target and substitutes the next sequence’s first input. This affects accepted layouts such as `batch: 4, virtual_gpus: 1`. In a CPU reproduction with two previously observed sequences, zero model losses and `budget_theta: 1`, the correct budgets were eight 64s; this implementation returned 128 for one token.
- fix: Reconstruct each sequence separately by appending its own final target to its inputs. Score the resulting batch, then flatten the losses. Add a multi-sequence budget regression.

### 2. [minor] E15’s diagnostic readouts remain inside the training clock
