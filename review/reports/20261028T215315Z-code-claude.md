# code review (with the research lens on E5 and E12): Claude

- source: frozen copy `/var/tmp/loud-review/20261028T215315Z-code/src` (= commit 95196d5 plus the staged `review/codex.sh`), scope 1ef9656..95196d5
- scratch evidence: `/tmp/claude-0/-mnt-disk-loud/4bad57c0-2fd8-46ee-bf11-6bbc28f617aa/scratchpad/review4/` (`bin/e5_real.py`, `e5_real_128.json`)

**Verdict.** `credit_recovery` does the same arithmetic as PR #40's chain. Its float32 and bf16 paths are right: I checked them numerically on a trained checkpoint. But E5 as configured measures a launch that cannot work at the bottom sites, for a structural reason the plan did not foresee. On the real checkpoint the cosine at mid0 to mid2 is 0.83 to 0.92 whatever the ridge. A launch with x0 held recovers 0.9999 at every mid site, so the decision rule would drop a variant that works. The multi-GPU plumbing is sound, but adding `gpus` to `config_hash` breaks resubmission of all 48 jobs in the live queue. The E12 re-tunes run one seed, with no way to run more. The README was not updated for any of this commit.

### 1. [major] E5's embedding launch is biased by x0's skip paths, and the configured decision cannot see it; a launch at mid0 with x0 held recovers every mid site
- where: `loud/diagnostics.py:361-364` (`dx0 = norm(e + v) - x0`, then `lam_0[b] * dx0` at every block); `configs/e5/recovery_c20.yaml`, `recovery_c100.yaml` (decision text)
- evidence:
  - The probe at the embedding moves x0, which all 8 blocks read. The x0 lambdas reach 6.9 on a trained model: on `track1_boostcut_final.pt` they are `[0.79, 6.94, 2.01, 3.46, 1.38, 3.36, 1.22, 2.87]`.
  - So d = g_k·Δ_k + h_k·Δx0, and the 512 × 512 solve returns g_k + J_k^{-T}(...)h_k, not g_k. At mid0 the bias is exactly h_0/(λ_r0+λ_00), because Δ_0 is collinear with Δx0.
  - I ran the tool unchanged on CPU: the record checkpoint, held-out row 0, 128 tokens, with dense attention as in `method_checks`. Median cosine with `direct`, at ridge 0 / 1e-3 / 0.1:

    | site | tool, float32 | tool, bf16 ridge 1e-3 |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | mid0 | 0.830 / 0.830 / 0.827 (p10 0.77) | 0.830 |
    | mid1 | 0.890 / 0.891 / 0.897 | 0.891 |
    | mid2 | 0.918 / 0.920 / 0.927 | 0.920 |
    | mid3 | 0.951 / 0.952 / 0.963 | 0.953 |
    | mid4 | 0.963 / 0.965 / 0.972 | 0.965 |
    | mid5 | 0.988 / 0.987 / 0.986 | 0.987 |
    | mid6 | 0.993 / 0.992 / 0.990 | 0.992 |
    | mid7 | 0.999 / 0.998 / 0.996 | 0.998 |

  - The ridge changes nothing at the bottom, so the loss is not conditioning.
  - The same `changes` arithmetic, launched at mid0 with Δx0 = 0 and v = σ·RMS(mid0)·basis, gives a median cosine of at least 0.99996 (float32) and 0.9999 (bf16) at all eight mid sites at ridge 0 (p10 at least 0.9986 and 0.9972). This is the chain's own mid0 root read at the later sites. It also validates the MLP, relu² and head arithmetic, which the method check never exercises (#7).
  - The configs' rule covers only "fails at the top two or three sites". These numbers fail at the bottom three, which the rule would read as a hybrid in the wrong place or as "drop".
  - Caveat: the record's 10M checkpoint, not `e1.bp_b4`'s (none exist yet), and 128 tokens.
- fix: in `credit_recovery`, add a second reading: the launch at mid0 with x0 held (delta = v at mid0; no `lam_0 * dx0` term), solved the same way. Report both. Then extend the decision text: an embedding cycle plus the mid0 launch give all nine sites from 2 cycles instead of 9, under the same rules question.

### 5. [minor] `credit_recovery`'s condition number is the RMS-norm radial artefact, and it is the number the E5 table reports
- where: `loud/diagnostics.py:394-395`; `loud/plots.py:168` (`bf16 condition`)
- evidence:
  - The embedding's RMS norm removes the radial direction to first order, so s_min of A is a second-order effect of σ.
  - On the record checkpoint the tool reports a median condition of 249 at mid0, where the map with x0 held has condition 1.00. It reports 960 to 1250 at every other site, against 43 to 807 for the map from mid0.
  - `token_map_svd` reports `condition_without_radial` for this reason; this tool does not.
- fix: also record `s[:, 0] / s[:, -2]`, and put that in the plan's 9 × 2 table.

### 6. [minor] The ridge, scaled by mean(diag AᵀA), pulls every direction with condition above 1/√ridge toward the identity guess
- where: `loud/diagnostics.py:397-401`
- evidence:
  - λ = ridge·mean(s²) shrinks any direction with s² < λ. At 1e-3 that means condition above 32; at 0.1, above 3.
  - In the x0-held launch (#1), ridge 1e-3 lowers the median cosine from 0.99998 to 0.9936 to 0.9984 at mid3 to mid7, and ridge 0.1 lowers it to 0.975 to 0.99.
  - The only direction that needs the ridge in the embedding launch is the radial one (s ≈ σ·s_typ, so s² ≈ 1e-4·s_typ²).
- fix: use ridges around 1e-5 to 1e-4, or scale λ by an estimate of the finite-difference error rather than by the mean diagonal.

### 9. [minor] `credit_recovery` spends its time on SVDs it does not need
- where: `loud/diagnostics.py:386-404`
- evidence:
  - Per checkpoint: 2 precisions × 128 chunks × 9 sites × 16 tokens = 36,864 `svdvals` of 512 × 512, plus 110,592 LU solves.
  - The chain arithmetic is about 80 TFLOP, which is seconds on an H100. cuSOLVER runs SVDs larger than 32 × 32 one matrix at a time, so the SVDs dominate. My estimate is minutes to tens of minutes (unverified: no GPU).
  - At emb, A is σ·RMS·(the signed basis): its condition is 1, and the solve is basisᵀd / (step·W), both known in closed form.
  - The float32 condition repeats `token_map_svd`.
- fix: take one `torch.linalg.svd(A)` per site and derive the condition and every ridge solution from it: c = V diag(s/(s²+λ)) Uᵀd + V diag(λ/(s²+λ)) Vᵀg. Skip the SVD and solve at emb.

## Checked and fine

- **Chain arithmetic.** `credit_recovery`'s `changes` against `chain_chunk_eager(site=-1)` (PR #40): `(lam_r[0]+lam_0[0])·dx0`, then `lam_r·δ + lam_0·dx0`; the relu² difference `(up−down)(up+down)`; bf16 casts at the same products; the float32 target logit; the head norm through `rsqrt(mean((xL+δ)²)+eps)`; the 15·tanh cap; logsumexp.
  - The basis is PR #40's `basis()` (Hadamard × column signs). The step is σ·RMS(e) per token. `solve(A, d)` equals the chain's `derivative @ H / W`.
  - Numerically correct in both precisions (#1, x0-held launch).
- **Comparison target.** `direct` (grad of the token's own loss, attention frozen, x0 held, all units) is what PR #40's direct cycle at a mid site measures (`site >= 0` → `dx0 = None`). It is the right target; `direct_vs_full` gives the cut's own gap (0.59 at mid0 on the record checkpoint).
